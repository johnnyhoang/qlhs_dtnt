Purpose

This overview page gives a quick operational summary of the student management area. It is designed for fast situational awareness before you open a detailed module.

What to look at

  • Total students: the overall size of the current managed student population.
  • Meal cut-offs today: how many students have meal exceptions reported for the current day.
  • Transport support: how many students currently have transport support records.
  • Latest payment batch: the most recent support payment cycle shown on the dashboard.

Using this page

  1. Start here if you need a quick status check across major QLHS functions.
  2. Review the statistics for unusual values or missing updates.
  3. Open the reporting tabs to inspect trend data and internal summaries.
  4. Move to the detailed modules from the group navigation when action is needed.
