Purpose

This page stores and maintains health insurance information for each student. It supports card number management, validity dates, registration details, and administrative corrections.

Important areas

  • Main table: shows insurance card number, expiry date, photo submission status, and audit information.
  • Search and class filters: help you isolate a class or a specific student before editing.
  • Insurance drawer: contains card metadata, registration location, VssID lookup, address, and correction notes.
  • Photo submission block: records whether the required photo has been submitted and why it may still be missing.

How to use this page

  1. Open a student record and verify the card number and expiry date first.
  2. Use the date fields consistently to avoid reporting errors.
  3. Capture correction information when the printed card and the expected data do not match.
  4. Track the photo status because it is often required for completion of the insurance file.
  5. Use export if you need to submit or cross-check the insurance list externally.
