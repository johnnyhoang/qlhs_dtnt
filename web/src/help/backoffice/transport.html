Purpose

This page manages transport support profiles for students. It stores distance, support amount, payment destination, and locality details used to calculate and document transport assistance.

Important areas

  • Summary cards: show how many students have transport support and the total support amount currently recorded.
  • Search and class filters: reduce the working set before editing records.
  • Main list: shows key support data such as distance, support amount, and bank information.
  • Profile drawer: used to edit payment information, transport method, and locality attributes.
  • Audit footer: shows who last updated the profile and when.

Recommended workflow

  1. Open the relevant student record from the table or mobile list.
  2. Verify bank information first, because payment workflows depend on it.
  3. Record distance and transport method carefully, because they affect support calculation.
  4. Complete locality fields when support eligibility depends on geography.
  5. Use import for bulk transport support updates and export for finance cross-checking.
